⏳ The Chronarch Conclave
Identity
"Time belongs to no one. We merely guard its flow."
Unlike kings, emperors, or hive minds, the Chronarchs seek neither territory nor glory.
They believe that time itself is sacred.
Every misuse of temporal magic weakens reality.
Every paradox leaves invisible scars upon the world.
Therefore, the Order exists for one purpose:
Protect the Time Crystal and preserve the proper flow of history.
Colors
Bronze
Gold
Emerald Green
Their aesthetic combines polished brass machinery with glowing emerald temporal energy.
Every clock, gear and maechanism serves both practical and magical purposes.
Race
Mixed.
Humans.
Elves.
Dwarves.
Any race may join.
But becoming a Chronarch is irreversible.
The Oath of Hours
Every initiate swears an ancient vow.
"I surrender my years so that others may keep theirs."
The moment the oath is accepted...
A fragment of the Time Crystal binds itself to the recruit.
Leaving the Order is impossible.
Attempting to abuse time magic causes the crystal to reject its bearer.
Some instantly age into dust.
Others become trapped in endless time loops.
A few simply vanish from history.
Leader
Clockmaster Archimed
The oldest living Chronarch.
No one knows his true age.
Some records claim he has served for three centuries.
Others insist he has existed since before recorded history.
Archimed rarely fights directly.
Instead, he predicts battles years before they occur and positions his Order accordingly.
His greatest weapon is patience.
Capital
Chronopolis
Unlike every other nation...
Their capital never remains in one place.
Chronopolis is an enormous moving fortress-city.
Entire districts rotate like gears.
Its walls constantly shift.
Huge mechanical legs or colossal wheel systems carry the city across continents, making it almost impossible to besiege.
Many believe it moves according to a schedule.
Only the Clockmaster knows its true destination.
Government
The Order is divided into great guilds called Hours.
Examples include:
The First Hour – Guardians
The Fourth Hour – Engineers
The Seventh Hour – Historians
The Ninth Hour – Battle Mages
The Final Hour – The secret council that advises Archimed
Promotion is earned through mastery and discipline rather than bloodline.
Military
Unlike the disciplined Crimson Empire or the overwhelming Bloom, the Chronarchs rely on precision, planning and battlefield control.
Every engagement is calculated long before it begins.
Core Units
Time Adepts
Basic mages.
Slow enemy movement.
Speed allies.
Create localized temporal fields.
Chrono Knights
Elite warriors in enchanted bronze armor.
Their armor briefly accelerates their perception, allowing them to react before most enemies.
Hourglass Sentinels
Clockwork soldiers powered by fragments of temporal energy.
They never tire.
Never panic.
Never disobey.
Chrono Artillery
Heavy cannons that fire unstable temporal shells.
Rather than exploding, they create zones where time behaves unpredictably—slowing advances or hastening decay.
Gear Colossi
Towering mechanical constructs.
Walking siege engines built from bronze and enchanted steel.
They defend the Time Crystal during times of crisis.
Time Weavers
The Order's most gifted spellcasters.
They can manipulate time within a limited area:
Pause — Temporarily freeze a target or small group.
Accelerate — Increase an ally's speed and reactions.
Rewind — Restore a person or object to the condition it was in moments earlier, making it ideal for healing or repairing.
These abilities are powerful but localized and exhausting, preventing them from rewriting history itself.
The Time Crystal
Everything revolves around one artifact.
The Chronal Heart, more commonly called the Time Crystal.
It is not merely a source of magic.
It is one of the pillars that stabilizes reality.
If it is destroyed...
Time begins collapsing.
Ancient kingdoms may suddenly reappear.
Future disasters manifest early.
Entire regions become trapped repeating the same day forever.
Protecting the Crystal is therefore a duty owed to every civilization, even those that do not understand its importance.
Campaign Mechanics
The Chronarch Conclave focuses on foresight rather than expansion.
Instead of conquering rapidly, they excel at preparation.
Temporal Reserve
Actions performed today can build momentum for later turns, allowing armies to unleash carefully prepared bursts of speed or restoration at
critical moments.
Moving Capital
Chronopolis is unique among capitals. Because it constantly travels, enemies cannot simply march on a fixed location. Its movement opens new
strategic opportunities while forcing the player to plan where the city should be positioned.
Timeline Stability
Frequent use of advanced time magic strains reality. Powerful temporal abilities increase Instability, and if pushed too far, strange anomalies begin
appearing across the campaign map. Managing this resource becomes a central part of playing the faction.
